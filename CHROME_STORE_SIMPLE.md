# ZEPP Saver - Smart Price Comparison for Students

**Save money while shopping online! Compare prices across Amazon, Flipkart, and Nykaa with exclusive student discounts from ZEPP.**

## What is ZEPP Saver?

ZEPP Saver is your ultimate shopping companion that automatically compares prices between major e-commerce platforms and ZEPP's student discount program. Get the best deals on products while enjoying exclusive student benefits!

### Key Features

**Automatic Price Comparison**
- Real-time price comparison between Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa, and ZEPP
- Instant savings calculation displayed in Indian currency format (₹24,00,000)
- Smart price tracking across different product categories

**Student Authentication**
- Secure student email verification with OTP
- Access to exclusive ZEPP student discounts
- One-time login with persistent sessions

**Smart Gift Card Recommendations**
- Personalized gift card offers based on your shopping behavior
- Platform-specific discount opportunities
- Exclusive deals for authenticated students

**Cart-Level Analysis**
- Bulk price comparison for your entire shopping cart
- Total savings calculation across multiple items
- Optimized shopping recommendations

**Intelligent UI**
- Clean, non-intrusive popups that don't disrupt your shopping
- Skeleton loading for smooth user experience
- Responsive design that works on all screen sizes

## How It Works

1. **Browse Normally**: Shop on Amazon, Flipkart, or Nykaa as usual
2. **Automatic Detection**: Extension detects products and extracts pricing
3. **Student Login**: Verify your student status with academic email + OTP
4. **Price Comparison**: See real-time price differences and potential savings
5. **Smart Shopping**: Choose the best deal or access exclusive student discounts

## Privacy & Security

**We respect your privacy and follow strict data protection practices:**

### Data Collection
- **Authentication Data**: Only your student email and login status
- **Product Information**: Product IDs and prices for comparison (not stored permanently)
- **Usage Analytics**: Anonymous usage patterns to improve service quality

### Data Storage
- **Local Storage Only**: Authentication status stored locally in your browser
- **No Personal Data**: We don't collect personal information beyond email verification
- **Secure Sessions**: All authentication uses industry-standard security practices

### Data Sharing
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers
- **API Communication**: Secure communication with ZEPP servers only for price comparison
- **Temporary Processing**: Product data processed in real-time, not stored

## Permissions Explained

**Why does ZEPP Saver need these permissions?**

### Active Tab Access
- **Purpose**: Read product information and prices from e-commerce sites
- **Usage**: Extract product IDs (ASIN, FSN, SKU) and current prices
- **Limitation**: Only accesses active tab when you're shopping

### Storage Permission
- **Purpose**: Remember your student authentication status
- **Usage**: Store login state to avoid repeated authentication
- **Data**: Only email and authentication status (no passwords stored)

### Host Permissions
- **script.google.com**: For price comparison API calls
- **localhost:3000**: Development and testing API endpoints
- **E-commerce domains**: Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa for price extraction

### Scripting Permission
- **Purpose**: Inject price comparison interface into shopping pages
- **Usage**: Display price comparisons and savings information
- **Safety**: Runs in isolated environment, cannot access other tabs
